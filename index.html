Yoga Tug of War 🧘

Two teams, 10 questions each. Name the yoga pose, read the posters, and know the benefits and body parts used. Every right answer pulls the rope to your side!

👥 Team names

⏱ Time for each question

📚 Choose questions

How to play:
  • Each team plays on its own half of the board and gets its own different questions.
  • Each team moves on to its next question as soon as it answers — no waiting for the other team.
  • Right answer = the rope moves 1 step to your side. Time up or wrong answer = no pull.
  • After 10 questions each, the team with more right answers wins. Equal marks = a draw.
